# Storefront direction: Mamoni Bosraloy

Traditional Bangladeshi clothing store feel: warm, trustworthy, colourful and mobile-first. Most customers browse on phones, so the Bangla UI, Bangla digits (৳১,২৯০) and large touch targets come first.

Tokens: maroon #7A1F2B (brand/buttons), gold #E8BF5A (accents/badges), cream #FBF6EE (page), ink #2B1A1A, sale red #C1272D, border #E6D9C6. Noto Sans Bengali for Bangla text and controls; Cormorant Garamond for English headlines.

Logo: a shapla (water lily, the national flower) mark in maroon and gold beside the wordmark: "মামণি বস্ত্রালয়" with "MAMONI BOSRALOY" as the sub-line (reversed in English mode). The same mark is public/images/brand-mark.svg (favicon). The wordmark is inline Blade (components/brand-logo) so it follows the active language. An uploaded Settings logo replaces it.

Layout: maroon announcement bar with hotline; sticky white header with pill search; maroon category navigation; hero slider; trust cards (delivery, COD, 7-day return); circular category row; featured, festival, offers and new-arrival grids; dark footer with a gold trust bar. Product cards show price, strike-through original price, % off badge, colour swatches and size range. On phones a bottom navigation (Home, Shop, Wishlist, Bag, Account), a sticky add-to-bag button and an optional WhatsApp button (Settings key `whatsapp`) are shown.

# Storefront direction

The database recommendation suggested liquid glass and a black palette. Those obscure product photography and form contrast, so this store uses an opaque, light layout instead.

Tokens: paper #FFFFFF, ink #202632, indigo #333D79, slate #626B79, mist #F2F4F8, border #D9DDE5. Cormorant Garamond for collection headlines; system sans and Noto Sans Bengali for controls and Bangla text. Content remains left aligned, with generous space around photography and compact shopping controls.

Layout: a quiet utility announcement, wordmark and search, category navigation, then a full-width image beside a large seasonal headline. A four-column product grid becomes two columns on mobile. Product pages pair photography with explicit size/color choices. Checkout uses a single-column form with a clear order summary. No decorative animation; visible focus and 44px touch controls.
